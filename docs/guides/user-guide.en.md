# User guide — Artaveo Dental

## A working day
1. **Sign in** with your user name and password. After a few idle minutes the screen locks; unlock with your password.
2. **New patient**: Patients → New patient. The program warns about a similar name or phone number (duplicate check).
3. **Appointments and reception**: Appointments to schedule; Reception for check-in and the waiting queue.
4. **Examination and dental chart**: in the patient file: the chart (FDI/Universal/Palmer), clinical notes, prescription.
5. **Treatment plan and treatment**: add services to the plan, mark them done as they are performed.
6. **Billing and payment**: an issued invoice cannot be edited (only voided or credited). Payments, installments, refunds.
7. **Receipt and printing**: compact A4, 58/80 mm thermal, or PDF.
8. **Daily cash closing** at the end of the day.
9. **Backup** runs by itself; see its state and the latest copy under Backup.

## Long forms
What you typed is kept as a draft. If the program closes or the power goes off, it offers to bring the draft back.

## Language, calendar, look
Top bar: Dari/Pashto/English, Shamsi or Gregorian calendar, light/dark theme.

## When something goes wrong
- An error shows a **reference** code: tell it to support.
- **System → Support report**: create the file and send it (it has no patient data).
- If the program does not open, **Safe mode** shows the way; your data is not deleted.
